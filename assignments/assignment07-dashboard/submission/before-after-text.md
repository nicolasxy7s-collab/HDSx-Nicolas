# Before and After Text

## Original Interpretation

For the selected income group, the table and figure describe the mean BMI pattern across available NHANES cycles in the prepared classroom dataset. The dashboard should help the audience ask better questions about pattern, timing, and context rather than treat the line as a causal explanation.

## Revised Interpretation

Each point is the average BMI of included adults aged 20-80 in the selected income group for one NHANES cycle; the table shows how many records contributed. These unweighted classroom summaries describe the included records and do not show that income caused BMI to change or that the pattern applies to all US adults.

The prepared dataset moves directly from the 2017-2018 cycle to the 2021-2023 cycle, with no intervening cycle included. Although the 2021-2023 label spans three calendar years, it represents approximately two years of collection, from August 2021 to August 2023 ([CDC documentation](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/continuousnhanes/overviewbrief.aspx?Cycle=2021-2023)). Cycle labels are equally spaced rather than positioned by elapsed time; the connecting line does not show intervening annual measurements or an annual rate of change. Each cycle summarizes a different sample of adults, rather than changes in the same individuals. The vertical scale adjusts to the selected group, so compare table values rather than apparent heights or slopes across group settings. No uncertainty intervals or statistical tests are shown.

## Why the change helps

The revision explains the chart points and table counts, distinguishes the cycle gap from the approximately two-year duration of the 2021-2023 cycle, and clarifies the limits of visual comparisons. No analytic logic or R code changed.
