# Figure Caption
This figure shows mean BMI (kg/m²) by income group across the available NHANES cycles using the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom dataset. The summary is descriptive and unweighted. Records with missing BMI, income group, or cycle were excluded, as were records outside ages 20–80; only cycle–income cells with at least 30 records are shown. The plot supports comparison of patterns, not causal claims or population-level estimates.
