# Provenance and Stewardship Note

The analysis uses the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom CSV, `examples/nhanes-equity/data/nhanes_equity_v6.csv`.

Its source is CDC/NCHS National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey public-use data.

The snapshot is accessed from the course workspace cache so the analysis can run offline without a fresh download.

This use supports descriptive classroom learning: the unweighted, non-causal summaries describe retained records and do not estimate national population values or individual health outcomes.

Report exclusions and aggregate results transparently, avoidid unsupported small-cell claims, and did not upload row-level health records to external AI tools or attempt participant identification.
The Week 7 guidance states that this snapshot does not include Indigenous identifiers.
